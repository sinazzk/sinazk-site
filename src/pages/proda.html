CASE STUDY 05 · 2020 · ENTERPRISE FIELD APP

Proda

An internal app that guides technicians through inspecting and repairing mobile network towers, and gives supervisors what they need to act.

MCI

ROLE
Product designer
CLIENT
NAK, for MCI (Mobile Communications of Iran)
TIMELINE
6 months, 2020
USERS
Field technicians · supervisors
AT A GLANCE

Problem. A rigorous back-and-forth between technicians reporting problems and supervisors deciding on action, which had to feel simple on a phone, on site.

What I did. Mapped the end-to-end process, explored many sketches, and tested several versions of the reporting and review flow until it was straightforward.

Outcome. A tested reporting and review flow that kept technicians and supervisors on one clear path, and my first deep dive into B2B.

DETAIL 01 · CONTEXT·SHEET 01/04

From site visit to supervisor decision

Proda is the internal app NAK technicians use to examine and repair base transceiver stations (BTS) for MCI. Technicians find the next site to visit, report problems on site, and supervisors review the details and photos to decide what to do.

USERS
Technicians in the field · supervisors in the office
SITES
Base transceiver stations across Tehran
DETAIL 02 · THE PROBLEM·SHEET 02/04

A complex process that had to feel obvious

The challenge was making every step of a rigorous back-and-forth feel intuitive to technicians, while giving supervisors a clear picture to act on. After many sketches and ideas, we reached a flow that was easy to understand and work with.

METHODS
Process mapping · sketching · wireframes
FOCUS
Issue reporting and review

Process map: site QA, from arrival to finishScroll sideways →

DETAIL 03 · THE SOLUTION·SHEET 03/04

Purely functional, tested many times

The reporting and review process was tested repeatedly with different designs. Because this is a working tool used in the field, the effort went into making the process as direct as possible rather than decorative.

PLATFORM
Android app for field teams
PRINCIPLE
Fewest steps from site to report
DETAIL 04 · OUTCOME & REFLECTION·SHEET 04/04

What I learned

Proda was my first B2B project, and it taught me how permission and role-based systems work: who can report, who can review, and what each person needs to see at each step.

That way of thinking still shapes how I design for teams inside organisations.

LEARNED
Designing permissions and roles for a B2B system
