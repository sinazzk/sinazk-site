CASE STUDY 03 · 2021 · FINTECH

Cryptoland

Redesigning onboarding, KYC and trading for one of Iran’s most popular cryptocurrency exchanges, across web and mobile, in two currencies.

ROLE
Product designer
CLIENT
Dadehpardaz
TIMELINE
6 months, 2021
PLATFORMS
Web app · iOS & Android
AT A GLANCE

Problem. Stakeholder and developer interviews revealed severe problems in onboarding, buying and selling, on a platform that mixed Iranian rials and dollars.

What I did. Built three trader personas, mapped the onboarding journey, re-ordered the information architecture through card sorting, and redesigned the core flows for web and mobile.

Outcome. Onboarding and referrals both improved after launch, compared with before the redesign.

+30% onboarding improvement
+24% referrals
3 trader personas guiding the redesign
DETAIL 01 · CONTEXT·SHEET 01/04

A fast-growing exchange with a painful first step

In 2020 Cryptoland was one of the most popular cryptocurrency platforms in Iran. Users deposited rials to buy tokens on the website, with a mobile app for frequent traders who wanted to buy and sell quickly from anywhere.

MARKET
Iran: rial deposits, crypto trading
USERS
First-time buyers to frequent traders
DETAIL 02 · RESEARCH·SHEET 02/04

Three kinds of trader, one confusing journey

From user data and extensive interviews I created three personas with different trading experience and strategies. Journey mapping exposed the pain points and set priorities; card sorting reshaped the site’s architecture, and other exchanges showed how similar problems had been solved.

METHODS
Interviews · personas · journey mapping · card sorting · competitor analysis
PRIORITY
Onboarding, KYC, buy & sell
PERSONA 01
First-time buyer. Needs to trust the platform before depositing.
PERSONA 02
Occasional investor. Wants clarity on fees and currencies.
PERSONA 03
Frequent trader. Wants speed on mobile.
DETAIL 03 · DESIGN·SHEET 03/04

Designing for two currencies at once

Everything from onboarding and KYC to deposits, withdrawals, buying and selling had to be designed meticulously, and the double-currency nature of the platform (IRR and USD) had to make sense at every step.

FLOWS
Onboarding · KYC · deposit · withdraw · buy · sell
CURRENCIES
IRR and USD side by side
DETAIL 04 · OUTCOME & REFLECTION·SHEET 04/04

What shipped, and what I learned

The redesigned UI and core flows shipped on web and mobile, and onboarding rose 30% and referrals 24% compared with the old product.

The bigger lesson was about trust. People come to a crypto exchange worried about losing real money, and good design starts by taking those worries seriously.

RESULT
+30% onboarding · +24% referrals, vs. before the redesign
