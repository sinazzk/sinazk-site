AT A GLANCE

Problem. Young people hunting for their first job rarely have anyone to practise with between programme sessions, and many are on low data plans with no appetite for another app.

What we did. We mapped the whole journey into conversation stages, designed the dialogue patterns and two coach personalities, and shaped the WhatsApp interface moments, validating each with AI engineers, consultants and researchers.

Outcome. Conversation flows, two coach personalities and UI concepts, handed over before my contract ended. The pilot later ran in Nigeria.

DETAIL 01 · CONTEXT·SHEET 01/07

Meet young people where they already talk

The King’s Trust Get Hired programme helps young people build interview skills and then meet employers who are hiring. Bain & Company was exploring how an AI coach could carry that support beyond the sessions: practising answers, writing a first CV, finding roles and keeping spirits up through rejections.

WhatsApp was the natural home. There is nothing to download, it copes with patchy connections, and voice notes let people practise speaking, not just typing.

DETAIL 02 · JOURNEY ARCHITECTURE·SHEET 02/07

Six phases, one continuous conversation

We mapped the whole relationship before writing a single message: onboarding, a learning programme that ends in a quiz and a certificate, live job hunting, and the weeks around starting work. Every stage carried notes for the AI team, such as “must clearly state the assistant is AI” or “stop learners tricking the tool into skipping modules”.

Laid out like this, one thing stood out. Emotional support is not a module, it is a thread: navigating emotions returns in five phases, and each time it can hand over to a real person.

DETAIL 03 · CONVERSATION DESIGN·SHEET 03/07

Teach by example, never leave anyone stuck

Most learners have never written a CV, so Sola asks first and lets the ones who have skip the basics. Every writing task then follows the same rhythm: show an example, ask for their version, nudge once, and if it still isn’t there, rewrite it with them and ask “how does that sound?”.

The aim is momentum. Nobody should finish a turn feeling they failed.

PATTERN
Model · attempt · re-prompt · rewrite
BRANCH
Prior CV experience

Practising out loud

Interviews are spoken, so practice is too. Learners answer in voice notes and get two specific pointers back. Once their answers are consistently clear, Sola offers a mock interview call with Kai, a second persona built for that pressure, and the debrief lands back in the chat.

DETAIL 04 · VOICE & PERSONALITY·SHEET 04/07

Two coaches, one voice

We wrote the personalities alongside the flows. Sola is the everyday coach: warm, patient and a little playful. Kai only appears for mock interviews and is more focused, closer to a friendly interviewer. Both share six tone pillars and a tone-by-scenario guide, so the AI engineers could tune responses moment by moment.

SolaLEARNING COACH · CHAT

Onboarding, modules, quizzes, job picks and check-ins. A seedling, because the whole programme is about growing into work.

KaiINTERVIEW COACH · CALLS

Mock interviews only. A distinct name and voice signal that this part is practice under pressure.

DETAIL 05 · INTERFACE·SHEET 05/07

Native WhatsApp moments, not a website in a chat

Long lists and forms don’t survive a chat window. We used WhatsApp’s own building blocks where they help: reply buttons for quick choices, Flows for the progress report, documents for anything worth keeping, and calls for mock interviews. Free text always still works.

DETAIL 06 · GUARDRAILS·SHEET 06/07

Safe by design for young users

Every message passes a few checks before the lesson carries on. Distress always goes to a human. Misuse gets a firm, respectful redirect. And because some learners will try to rush to the certificate, module order holds, with an explanation rather than a wall.

DETAIL 07 · CONCEPT TESTING·SHEET 07/07

Testing the hero moments with the team

We turned the three moments with the most potential into concept boards. Consultants, researchers and engineers dot-voted each one on four scales, then clustered what would create the most impact and what we needed to avoid.

ENGAGEMENTLEARNING SUPPORTRELATABILITYINCLUSIVITY

Personalised onboarding

An informal tone from the first message, deep-dive questions about goals, multiple-choice cards for preferences and steppers that show how far along you are.

Progress tracking

Ask for progress at any time, see each module with its completion, and collect badges tied to your own path.

AI coach

Emotional support and practical feedback for real situations, like a first day, plus step-by-step guides.

My contract ended before the pilot, which ran in Nigeria, so I didn’t see which concepts went forward. The boards above are where the team’s thinking stood when I handed over.
