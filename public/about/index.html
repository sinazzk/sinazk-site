SHEET 01 · ABOUT

From buildings to products

I started in architecture, with a BA at Imam Khomeini International University and an MA in Post-Disaster Studies at Shahid Beheshti University. Architecture taught me to see a building as a system of people, constraints and flows, and I still design software that way.

While studying, I started working as a designer at the Tehran Urban Innovation Centre, then in visual design at an advertising agency and an engineering firm. In 2019 I moved into UX at Dadehpardaz, designing enterprise services like the Cryptoland exchange and mentoring other designers.

In 2022 I began an MSc in User Experience Engineering at Goldsmiths in London. My thesis placement at Reason became Office Culture DJ, and London led to work with SuperReach, Conport Structures and Bain & Company on AI-powered learning, service blueprints and conversational interfaces.

Today I’m back in Tehran as head designer at TopTech, leading design for the tools its teams use every day and the products its customers use, and helping junior designers grow.

SHEET 02 · STORY

What I lead at TopTech

I head a team of seven product designers, and each of them owns a product: internal tools that TopTech’s own teams work in every day, including a multi-module ERP for the automotive sector, and the products its customers use.

My part is the system around them. I’m using AI to streamline how we work and how the design system is built and kept up, I mentor each designer as they grow, and I’m helping the company build a more open, collaborative design culture.
